**VCO Community Creator Content Specification**

## **Project Overview**
The **VCO Community Creator Area** is designed to expand the reach and diversity of content available on the VCO Media Database by allowing external creators to contribute and manage their own media content. This functionality ensures that creators have **exclusive control over their own content**, while **maintaining strict access limitations** to prevent unauthorized modifications of VCO Admin or other creators’ content. The new system will seamlessly integrate into the existing platform, providing an intuitive, feature-rich experience for both creators and viewers.

---

## **Core Requirements**

### **1. Creator & User Account System**
- **Registration & Approval Process for Creators:**
  - Creators apply for an account via a dedicated sign-up form.
  - VCO Admins review and approve applications manually to ensure quality and authenticity.
  - Once approved, creators receive login credentials and can begin creating events.
  
- **User Registration & Account Management:**
  - Users must register an account to purchase and use credits.
  - Accounts store **credit balance, purchase history, and downloads**.
  - Secure authentication via **OAuth-based login (Google, Apple, Email & Password)**.
  
- **Authentication & Access Control:**
  - **Role-based access control (RBAC)** ensures that creators can only access and manage their own events and content.
  - **Creators cannot access content uploaded by VCO Admins or other creators**.
  - VCO Admins have full moderation control and can remove inappropriate or non-compliant content.
  - Users only have access to purchased content but can browse free media.

---

### **2. Creator Admin Panel**
- **Event Management:**
  - Creators can **create, edit, and delete** their own events.
  - Each event can be assigned a **cover image**, **title**, and **description**.
  - Events are automatically categorized based on creation date and content type.

- **Media Upload & Management:**
  - **Bulk upload** support for both images and videos.
  - **Efficient metadata management**, allowing captions and tags to be added, edited, or deleted in bulk.
  - A **custom tagging system** for better content discoverability.
  
- **Visibility & Monetization Options:**
  - Creators decide per event whether content is **public** or **behind a paywall** (see Payment System section).
  - Admin panel displays an overview of event performance, including view counts and engagement metrics.

---

### **3. Homepage Integration for Creator Content**
- **Dedicated "Creator Content" Section:**
  - A highly visible **Creator Content Block** on the homepage to showcase **diverse and engaging** content.
  - Dynamic updates ensure that newly uploaded or trending content is highlighted.
  
- **Design & Navigation Options:**
  - **Option 1:** A dedicated **Creator Content Page**, structured similarly to the main homepage, allowing users to browse creator-uploaded events.
  - **Option 2:** Fully **integrate Creator Content** within the main homepage but clearly label it as “Community Creator Content.”
  - **Option 3:** A **carousel or grid display** that showcases a rotating selection of featured Creator Content.
  
- **Search & Filtering Capabilities:**
  - Users can filter content by **event name, creator, media type, or popularity**.
  - AI-powered **recommended content** dynamically suggests relevant media.

---

### **4. Payment & Credit System for Premium Content**
- **Credit-Based System:**
  - Users purchase **Credits**, where **1 Credit = 1 EUR = 1 Image or Video Download**.
  - Creators receive **70% of the revenue per Credit spent on their content (0.70 EUR per Credit)**.
  - Users manage their credit balance via their account.
  
- **Per-Event Paywall Option:**
  - Creators have the option to **enable or disable** paid access for each event.
  - Paid content is only accessible after sufficient credits are used.
  
- **Revenue Tracking & Payout Management:**
  - Each credit transaction is linked to a **specific event and creator**.
  - Admins have access to an **earnings report dashboard** that provides insights into revenue per creator and event.
  
- **Subscription Model:**
  - Users can opt for a **monthly subscription** where their credits automatically **reset to a fixed amount each month**.
  - Subscription tiers determine how many credits a user receives monthly.
  - Unused credits do not carry over.
  
- **Payment Processing:**
  - Integration with secure **payment providers (Stripe, PayPal, or similar)**.
  - Supports **one-time credit purchases** and **subscription-based models**.
  
- **VCO Admin Oversight:**
  - Admins can **review financial reports**, manage payouts, and ensure compliance with financial regulations.
  - Automated system flags any discrepancies or refund requests for manual review.

---

## **Future Enhancements (Integrated into Specification)**

- **Subscription Model for Creators:**
  - Fans can **subscribe** to specific creators for **exclusive access** to their premium content.
  - Subscription-based payments provide a **recurring revenue model** for both creators and the platform.

- **Analytics Dashboard for Creators:**
  - Creators receive **detailed engagement metrics**, including:
    - Number of views per media item.
    - Download statistics.
    - Earnings breakdown per event.
  - Allows creators to **optimize content** based on performance insights.

- **AI-Powered Content Tagging:**
  - **Automated tagging system** uses machine learning to categorize content more effectively.
  - AI-powered recognition helps **identify objects, race tracks, and team branding**.
  - Improves **searchability** and enables better **content recommendations**.

---

## **Technical Considerations**
- **Authentication & Security:**
  - **Role-based access control (RBAC)** to ensure strict content isolation.
  - **OAuth-based authentication** to provide secure login and access management.
  
- **Storage & Media Optimization:**
  - Utilizes **AWS S3, Cloudinary, or similar services** for secure and scalable media storage.
  - Implements **lazy loading** and **CDN caching** to optimize performance.

- **Performance Optimization:**
  - **Adaptive bitrate streaming** for videos ensures smooth playback on all devices.
  - **Optimized image formats (WebP, AVIF)** reduce bandwidth usage.

- **Payment Security & Compliance:**
  - Ensures **GDPR and PCI-DSS** compliance for handling sensitive payment data.
  - Supports multi-currency transactions for **international accessibility**.

---

## **Conclusion**
This **Community Creator Integration** expands VCO’s media platform into a **scalable, high-quality, and monetizable** system that enables creators to contribute their content while maintaining **strict content security and access control**. The addition of **paid content, a credit-based economy, analytics tools, and AI-powered features** ensures that both VCO and independent creators can **benefit from a professional and profitable media-sharing environment**.
