VCO Content Database – Branded Email Templates

Please replace the current plain-text account verification email with a premium, branded HTML email that matches the existing VCO Content Database website.

1. Inspect First

Review the current Resend integration, email verification flow, existing branding and any reusable email templates.

Reuse the existing email infrastructure. Do not introduce another email provider or authentication system.

2. Visual Design

Create a modern, motorsport-inspired email design consistent with the actual website:

* Dark, premium visual appearance
* Existing VCO logo, brand colours and typography where email-compatible
* Strong headline and clear hierarchy
* Prominent primary CTA button
* Subtle motorsport-inspired styling
* Clean spacing and mobile-friendly layout
* Professional footer

Avoid generic corporate email templates and excessive decorative elements.

Use email-compatible HTML with inline styles and a readable plain-text alternative.

3. Verification Email

Subject: Verify your email | VCO Content Database

Headline: One click. You’re in.

Body:
Welcome to the VCO Content Database.

Confirm your email address to activate your account and explore motorsport photography, racing moments and creator galleries.

Primary CTA:
VERIFY EMAIL ADDRESS

Additional information:

* Verification link expires after 60 minutes.
* Link can only be used once.
* If the recipient did not request registration, they can ignore the email.

4. Security and Functionality

Preserve the existing verification token, expiry and single-use behaviour.

* Do not change the authentication logic.
* Do not expose verification tokens in logs.
* Use the existing trusted PUBLIC_SITE_URL.
* Keep verification URLs out of unnecessary tracking or redirect systems.
* Do not add marketing tracking pixels.
* Ensure the CTA works across common email clients.
* Include a fallback verification link in case the button fails.
* Never include a user’s password or other credentials.

5. Reusable Template

Create one lightweight reusable email layout for future transactional emails, such as:

* Email verification
* Password reset
* Welcome email
* Account notifications

Only implement the verification email now.

Do not create a complex email template management system.

6. Validation

Test rendering on desktop and mobile, including dark and light email clients.

Confirm that:

* Resend accepts the email.
* The sender uses RESEND_FROM.
* The button points to the correct environment.
* The verification process still works.
* The plain-text alternative remains usable.

Use an authorized test recipient and avoid unnecessary production emails.

Do not deploy without approval.

Final Report

Briefly summarize the template changes, branding decisions, test results and any remaining limitations.

Priority: A beautiful, reliable VCO-branded email with minimal implementation complexity.
