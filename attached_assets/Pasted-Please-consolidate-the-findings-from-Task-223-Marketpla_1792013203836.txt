Please consolidate the findings from:

* Task #223 — Marketplace decisions and specification
* Task #224 — Professional image metadata workflows

into ONE coherent implementation specification for the VCO Content Marketplace.

This is a PLANNING-ONLY task. Do not implement, migrate, deploy or change Production.

Approved product principles:

1. Extend the existing VCO Content Database rather than replacing it.
2. Creator plans (Free, Pro, Studio, Archive) are separate from application roles.
3. Creators manage one unified storage quota across original photos, RAW, videos, previews, persistent variants and Creator-managed sidecars.
4. RAW files do not require a special subscription or pricing model.
5. IPTC/XMP metadata is optional for all Creators and must never be a general upload requirement.
6. Existing Creator metadata takes precedence. Missing eligible fields can be filled using event data, AI tagging, matching and captioning.
7. Never infer authorship, copyright ownership or licensing rights from AI or uploader identity.
8. Preserve all uploaded originals byte-for-byte, including official VCO uploads.
9. Use non-destructive JPEG metadata exports and XMP sidecars for RAW.
10. Offer privacy-filtered standard downloads. Exact-original downloads require explicit Creator availability settings and warnings about sensitive embedded metadata.
11. Use a bounded, automatically cleaned platform-managed cache for generated downloads. Temporary cache objects must not unexpectedly consume Creator storage allowances. Persistent Creator-managed variants and sidecars do count.
12. Buyer credits and Creator monetary earnings require separate auditable ledgers.
13. Purchases must create durable licence entitlements independent of download counts.
14. Stripe subscriptions, payments and Connect payouts must be implemented securely, with reconciliation and idempotent webhook handling.
15. No VCO-financed Creator assignments or VCO-funded RAW storage exceptions.

Please produce:

A. Unified target architecture and data model.
B. Exact reuse versus required changes in existing components.
C. Phased implementation plan with dependencies and acceptance criteria.
D. Migration and backwards-compatibility strategy.
E. Storage quota and export-cache accounting rules.
F. Asset variants, RAW handling and metadata provenance design.
G. Licensing, financial ledger, subscriptions and payout architecture.
H. Security, privacy, operational and legal review requirements.
I. Open commercial decisions that must be approved before implementation.

Keep the implementation plan incremental, Production-safe and compatible with existing users, galleries and media.

Do not treat proposed commercial prices, licence terms or payout rules as final approvals.

Return the specification for review before any development starts.
