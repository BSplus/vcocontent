---
name: Image sharing boundaries
description: Operator-approved scope of social image sharing and platform verification claims.
---

Social sharing is limited to anonymously public, non-premium image previews,
video posters and gallery thumbnails. It must not add original/RAW/video file
handoff, posting integrations, account connections or publication permissions.
Creator-profile avatar and cover handoffs are excluded.

**Why:** The operator approved image-aware sharing without new access exceptions,
reuse rights or automatic posting. A successful native handoff is not evidence
of a published social post.

**How to apply:** Keep future sharing work within those boundaries unless
separately approved. Treat actual phone/app compatibility and external cached
link previews as unverified until independently checked; local browser mocks
cannot prove either. Previously delivered public bytes cannot be withdrawn from
another app's memory or cache.
