---
name: Media credit boundaries
description: Operator's authorship rules for uploads, Nextcloud sources and future profile integrations.
---

Credits identify the photographer or videographer, independently of the uploading account and tags. Empty credits must remain optional; do not automatically fill them from usernames or Creator profiles.

**Why:** The operator explicitly distinguishes the author from the uploader and excludes automatic profile-based attribution.

**How to apply:** Preserve this distinction when extending uploads, imports, search or public profiles. Source-credit edits apply only to future new media; they must not replace manually edited credits or change an already running import's attribution.
