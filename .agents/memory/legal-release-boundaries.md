---
name: Legal release boundaries
description: Operator approval boundaries for media rights and creator terms
---

Do not infer a private, editorial or commercial reuse licence from the free beta,
download availability or the main VCO site's legal texts. New usage rights and
binding creator terms require explicit operator decisions and legal review.

**Why:** The authorised legal-text adaptation deliberately leaves those licence,
age, contract and termination decisions unconfirmed; publishing a review draft
must not silently decide them for the operator.

**How to apply:** When introducing creator/community features, consult the legal
review documents for unresolved approvals. Do not use a Privacy Policy agreement
checkbox as blanket consent or retroactively treat existing creators as accepting
new terms.
