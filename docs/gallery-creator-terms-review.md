# Gallery & Creator Terms — REVIEW DRAFT, NOT IN FORCE

Version: 10 October 2026. English working text for operator and legal review.
**Not published as contractual terms, not attached to registration, not accepted
by existing users and not a grant of rights.** Bracketed items require decisions.
This draft is not legal advice or a compliance approval.

## 1. Service and operator

The VCO Media Database is operated by Virtual Competition Organisation GmbH,
Lindwurmstraße 88, 80337 München, Germany. Contact: info@vco-esports.com.
It presents VCO and community racing photography and authorised video material.
The creator beta provides free, limited photo-gallery creation and management.
Public browsing does not require an account; some playback and downloads do.

## 2. Accounts and eligibility

Use accurate account information and protect your login credentials. Creator
self-activation requires email verification and a safety-checked public profile.
Accounts already rejected or suspended cannot self-activate.

## 3. Upload responsibility and third-party rights

Upload only material you are entitled to provide. Check copyright, photographer
credits, personality/image rights, event permissions and rights in reference
lists. Do not imply that upload permission automatically permits public reuse.
Avoid unnecessary personal or sensitive information and do not upload passwords
or confidential material. If people are identifiable, establish the appropriate
legal basis for publication and information obligations.

[APPROVAL: workable proof-of-rights process, treatment of event/accreditation
restrictions, trademarks and disputed rights; whether VCO may publish material
provided by another uploader under that person’s authorisation.]

## 5. Download and reuse rights

Current confirmed public notice: copyright and third-party rights remain reserved.
Free download availability does not grant a licence to publish, redistribute or
commercially use material. Uses beyond statutory permissions require the
respective rights holder’s permission. Contact VCO for VCO material.

[DECISION: whether private, editorial and/or commercial reuse will be offered;

Permission from VCO cannot automatically cover a third-party creator’s rights.]

## 6. Beta scope and limits

The standard community beta currently allows 500 MB (500,000,000 bytes),
five galleries, 1,000 photos and 5 MB (5,000,000 bytes) per incoming photo.
New community video uploads are unavailable. Authorised official/admin paths
have a separate media policy. Storage includes references, reserved capacity
and pending deletion; it is not just the sum of visible gallery photos.
No purchases, subscriptions, payouts or earnings are active.

[APPROVAL: quota changes/exceptions, notice periods, duration/ending of beta,
availability and export options. Do not promise permanent free hosting or
preservation beyond the actual service.]

## 7. AI and private references

Media organisation and independent publication safety checks use Replit-managed
OpenAI. Relevant images, sampled video frames, text and reference images/scanned
pages may be transmitted. Private reference sources are owner/admin-restricted,
but derived registered lineups may become public media metadata.
AI matches are fallible and do not prove physical driver identity.
The Privacy Policy explains the processing; it is not blanket consent.

[APPROVAL: legal bases, rights in documents, risk assessment, separate disclosure
to people named/shown, correction and objection handling. No unverified
no-training, zero-retention or EU-only-processing promise.]

## 8. Prohibited material and present moderation

The current safety policy rejects sexualised content/pornography,
racist or dehumanising statements/depictions and hate symbols or glorification.
Actual or perceived protected traits of people are not reasons for rejection.
New or changed public content must pass separate text/image checks.
Uncertain, failed or incomplete checks keep it private; clearly prohibited
results block publication. Suitable uncertain results can receive documented
administrator review; technical failures/prohibited results cannot be approved
through that control. Administrators can hide galleries and suspend accounts.
Historical public material is not automatically reviewed.

[APPROVAL: further unlawful content, harassment, threats, intellectual-property
violations, misleading metadata, enforcement proportionality, warnings, emergency
action, repeat misuse, policy-change notices and human appeal of false positives.
The existing admin control is not a complete appeal pathway for all outcomes.]

## 9. Reports, reasons and challenges — PROCEDURE TO BE IMPLEMENTED

Candidate future procedure: an accessible electronic report identifies the
precise content/location, explains alleged illegality, supplies contact
information as required and includes a good-faith statement. The service
acknowledges receipt and communicates a reasoned outcome. Affected uploaders
receive a specific statement of reasons for a restriction, including relevant
facts, rule/legal basis, automation involvement, duration/scope and remedies.
Challenges are reviewed by appropriately authorised humans.

[LAUNCH GAP: classify under DSA; confirm Arts. 11/12 contact points and language;
implement Art. 16 including applicable exceptions to reporter identification,
receipt and decision notifications; Art. 17 statements of reasons and Art. 18
escalation; assess Arts. 19/20/21/24/28 and any applicable exemptions individually.
An info@ email link alone is **not** evidence this process exists. Do not publish
guaranteed response times or independent review before staffing them.]

## 10. Removal, termination and changes — DECISIONS REQUIRED

Current product: creator controls can unpublish/remove content; unpublishing is
not deletion. Storage cleanup can fail and is retried. No self-service account
erasure or complete coordinated deletion workflow exists. Rights requests can
be addressed to dataprotection@vco-esports.com.

[DECISIONS: creator termination, VCO termination grounds/notice, export window,
effects on published works and operating permission, references, pending review,
backup/provider copies and claims; no retroactive acceptance or deletion.
Legal review must address liability, mandatory consumer rights, changes to terms,
dispute routes and language without generic blanket liability exclusions.]

Obtain operator decisions on sections 1–10, legal approval of rights/contract
language, evidence of operational procedures, an implementation plan for any new
